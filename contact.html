    Before you ask

    Common questions

    How far in advance should I book?

    The sooner the better, especially for weekends in December and over the festive season. That said, we would always rather you asked about a short-notice date than assumed it was gone.

    Do you travel outside Alberton?

    Yes. We work right across Ekurhuleni — Palmridge, Katlehong, Germiston, Vosloorus — and into greater Gauteng. Travel is quoted with the rest of the job so there is nothing added afterwards.

    Can I hire items without styling?

    Absolutely. Browse the hire catalogue, send us the list, and we will quote delivery, set-up and collection on their own.

    Do you set up and pack down?

    Always. Our team delivers, builds on site before guests arrive, and comes back to strike everything down afterwards. You never carry a chair.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on guest count, venue and how much structure is needed, so we quote each event individually and itemise it. Send your date through and you will have a figure to work with.
