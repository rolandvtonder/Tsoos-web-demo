What we do

Four ways we dress a day.

Decor and styling, tent and draping, furniture hire and full set-up — booked separately or together as one package.

Service 01

Event Decor & Styling

The visual language of your day: colour palette, focal backdrop, table styling and all the small staged moments guests photograph without being asked.

  • ◆ Arch & panel backdrops
  • ◆ Balloon garlands & installations
  • ◆ Plinths, platforms & thrones
  • ◆ Tablescapes & centrepieces
  • ◆ Florals & candle staging
  • ◆ Welcome boards & signage
  • ◆ Cake & dessert tables
  • ◆ Themed party packs

Service 02

Tent & Draping

A lawn, a driveway or an open field becomes a proper room. We supply the structure, then soften every hard edge with draping until it reads as an interior.

  • ◆ Stretch tents
  • ◆ Frame marquees
  • ◆ Ceiling draping
  • ◆ Wall & leg draping
  • ◆ Side walls & screening
  • ◆ Artificial lawn & flooring

  • ◆ Gold bow-back chairs

Service 04

Full Event Set-Up

Everything above, run by one team from first WhatsApp to final pack-down.

01

Tell us the plan

Date, venue, guest count, theme and rough budget. A WhatsApp voice note is perfectly fine.

02

We design and quote

A styling direction plus an itemised quote — hire list, labour, delivery. No surprises later.

03

We deliver and build

Our team is on site early. Tent up, draping straight, tables laid, before the first guest arrives.

04

We strike it down

Packed, loaded and cleared afterwards. You never carry a chair or fold a table.

Pricing: every set-up is quoted on guest count, venue and how much structure is needed, so we have not published fixed package prices. Send us your date and we will come back with an itemised quote. If you would like standard packages on this page instead, we can add them.
