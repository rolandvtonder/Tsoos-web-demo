Who we are

A styling house built on showing up.

We are an event styling and hire company based in Palmridge, Alberton. From a single balloon arch to a fully draped marquee with seating for two hundred, we plan it, deliver it, set it up and strike it down — so the only thing you carry on the day is the occasion itself.

Birthdays. Baby showers. Weddings. Lobola celebrations. Unveilings and memorials. Whatever the moment calls for, it gets the same care.
