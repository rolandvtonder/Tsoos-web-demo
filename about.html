Our story

Built on showing up.

TSOOS Event Styling Entertainment is an event styling, decor and hire company working out of Palmridge in Alberton — and travelling wherever the celebration is.

Who we are

A name people pass on

Most of our work arrives the same way — someone saw a set-up at a cousin's birthday, or a friend's baby shower, and asked who did it. That is the whole business model, really. Do the job properly, and the next booking finds you.

We handle decor, furniture hire, tent and draping under one roof, which means one team, one quote, and one number to call when something needs adjusting the morning of. No chasing four different suppliers while you are meant to be getting dressed.

Our promise is on every piece of signage we own, and we mean it literally:

We Never Disappoint

What we stand for

Four things we refuse to cut

01

On time, always

We arrive with enough runway to build properly. A rushed set-up shows in the photographs forever.

02

Finish over flash

Straight draping, clean linen, matched glassware. The details guests never name are the ones they feel.

03

Any venue, any budget

A back garden in Palmridge or a hall in Germiston — we work with the space you have and price it honestly.

04

Every occasion counts

A first birthday gets the same attention as a wedding. So does a memorial. Nobody's day is a small job.
